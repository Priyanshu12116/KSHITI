How an answer is made

  1. ReadCerebras reads the question in under a second: language, topic, the citizen's numbers.
  2. CalculateCode looks up the figures in tables checked against the official PDF.
  3. CiteClaude writes a short answer from the 106 rules and cites the exact paragraph.
  4. CheckEvery quote is matched word for word. Unknown rule numbers are flagged.

Known limits

  • Rules consolidated to 06-03-2025. Later changes are flagged only when known.
  • Calculator covers residential setbacks, FAR, height band and fees. Other figures come from the cited text.
  • The Development Plan of a town may override these rules (Rule 103).
  • Source quotes are in English; the official Hindi PDF cannot be machine-read.
